Everything from this track on one page: a one-week preparation plan, a map from question to story, and a checklist for the day of the interview.

## The one-week plan

About an hour a day is enough if your stories are real.

| Day | Do | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Fill in the preparation grid from your resume; pick 8–10 stories | [Build Your Story Bank](/behavioral/foundations/story-bank) |
| **2** | Write each story in STAR, as notes, with a number in every result; tag the themes | [The STAR Method](/behavioral/foundations/star-method) |
| **3** | Write your 90-second pitch and your top project deep dive | [Tell Me About Yourself](/behavioral/foundations/tell-me-about-yourself), [Project Deep Dive](/behavioral/foundations/project-deep-dive) |
| **4** | Map your stories to the target company's values; fill the gaps | [Company Guides](/behavioral/companies/amazon) |
| **5** | Say every story out loud in 2 minutes; do ten questions in the drill; record two and review them against the red flags | [Answer Drill](/behavioral/question-bank/behavioral-drill), [Red Flags](/behavioral/playbook/red-flags) |
| **6** | Read the annotated mocks, then do a mock interview with a friend who asks follow-ups ("why?", "what exactly did you do?") | [Mock Interviews, Annotated](/behavioral/playbook/mock-behavioral-interviews) |
| **7** | Prepare why-this-company and five questions to ask; light review of the grid | [Motivation](/behavioral/foundations/motivation-questions), [Questions to Ask](/behavioral/foundations/questions-to-ask) |

## Question → theme → story

| If they ask… | Theme | Lead with a story about… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Disagreed with your manager / a peer" | Conflict | A technical or priority disagreement, resolved with data, or disagree-and-commit |
| "Led something", "influenced without authority" | Leadership | A change you drove across people you didn't manage |
| "Difficult teammate", "helped someone grow" | Collaboration | Understanding why, then making the work manageable; mentoring with an outcome |
| "Critical feedback", "learned something fast" | Growth | Feedback that changed a habit; how you learned |
| "Beyond your role", "unclear requirements" | Ownership | A problem nobody owned that you measured and fixed |
| "Biggest failure", "a mistake", "weakness" | Failure | A real mistake; half the story is the aftermath |
| "Tight deadline", "competing priorities" | Delivery | Cutting scope, not safety, and saying so early |
| "Proudest work", "used data to decide" | Impact | A measured outcome for users or the business |
| "Raised a concern", "asked to cut a corner" | Integrity | Raising a risk early through the right channel |
| "Tell me about yourself" | Pitch | Now → path → now in detail → why here |
| "Why us?", "why leave?" | Motivation | Something specific about them, true about you; toward, not away |

## The answer checklist

- [ ] Started with a one-line **nugget**.
- [ ] Situation and task in **under 30 seconds**.
- [ ] **Actions** numbered, with a "because" for each.
- [ ] **"I"** for my actions.
- [ ] **Result** with a number.
- [ ] **What I learned**, in one sentence.
- [ ] About **two minutes**; offered more detail instead of giving it all.

## Day-of checklist

- [ ] The story grid (keywords only) to glance at before you start.
- [ ] The company's values or principles, re-read.
- [ ] Your pitch said aloud once that morning.
- [ ] Five questions to ask, two in reserve.
- [ ] For video: camera at eye level, quiet room, notes not on screen in a distracting way.
- [ ] Water, and a few minutes of calm before each interview.

## Numbers to remember

| Item | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Stories in your bank | 8–10, including at least one failure and one conflict |
| Length of a STAR answer | About 2 minutes (Amazon suggests 2–3) |
| Share of the answer on Action | About 60% |
| Pitch ("tell me about yourself") | 60–90 seconds |
| Deep-dive projects | 2–3 known inside out |
| Questions to ask | Prepare 5, ask 2–3 |

<Takeaways>

- One week, an hour a day: grid → STAR → pitch and deep dive → company mapping → out loud → mock → closing questions.
- Map each question to a theme, and each theme to a story you've prepared.
- Check every answer: nugget, short situation, numbered actions with reasons, "I", a number, a lesson, about two minutes.
- Re-read the grid and the company values on the day; prepare questions to ask.

</Takeaways>
