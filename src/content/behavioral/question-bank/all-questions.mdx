## How to use this list

You don't need 152 answers. You need **8–10 stories** and the ability to see which story fits a question. So:

1. **Read a theme's list** and notice how many different wordings lead to the same underlying question.
2. **For each question, name the story** from your [story bank](/behavioral/foundations/story-bank) you'd use. Don't answer it fully; just pick.
3. **Mark the questions where no story fits.** That's a gap to fill.
4. **Answer five questions out loud each day**, mixing themes, in the [drill](/behavioral/question-bank/behavioral-drill).

<Callout type="tip" title="Recognise the theme from the wording">

- "Pushed back", "disagreed", "someone didn't agree with you" → **conflict**.
- "Without authority", "convinced", "got buy-in" → **leadership**.
- "Beyond your role", "unclear", "nobody owned" → **ownership**.
- "Wrong", "regret", "didn't go as planned", "weakness" → **failure**.

The wording changes; the theme doesn't. Spotting it in the first few seconds tells you which story to reach for.

</Callout>

## Hypothetical questions need a different shape

Hypotheticals ("What would you do if…") aren't stories, but answer them with structure:

1. **Clarify** the goal and the constraints.
2. **Give options** with their trade-offs.
3. **Choose**, and say why.
4. **Say how you'd measure it** and when you'd revisit it.
5. **Anchor in experience**: "When something similar happened on my team…".

See the [Google guide](/behavioral/companies/google) for a worked example.

<Practice task="Pick one theme. For each of its questions, write only the title of the story you would use. How many different stories did you need, and which question had no good fit?">

Most people find that two or three stories cover a whole theme of 12 questions. That confirms that a small, well-chosen story bank is enough. The questions with no fit usually reveal a specific missing story:

- "Tell me about a time you changed your mind after an argument" needs a conflict you **lost**.
- "Tell me about a risk that didn't pay off" needs a failure that came from a **deliberate** bet.

Add those to your bank.
