Every problem from every practice ladder in the [Problem-Solving Patterns](/dsa/patterns) course, in one place. Tick a problem when you've solved it **without looking at the answer**. The same checkboxes appear in each lesson's practice ladder and in the [study plan](/dsa/playbook/study-plan), so ticking it anywhere ticks it everywhere.

<Callout type="note" title="Where your progress is saved">

Progress is stored in this browser only. It survives reloads and new versions of the site, but not clearing site data, a private window or a different device.

## How to use it well

- **Tick only clean solves.** If you needed the answer, leave it unticked and re-solve it in a few days, using the [three-pass method](/dsa/start-here/how-to-practice).
- **Use the gaps.** Look for a pattern with no ticks after its week in the study plan, or no Hard ticked anywhere. That's the next thing to practise.
- **Use "Show unsolved only"** in the final week to pick unseen problems for mock interviews.

<Takeaways>

- One checklist for every practice problem, grouped by pattern, with counts by difficulty.
- Ticking a problem here, in a lesson or in the study plan updates all of them.
- Tick clean solves only; unticked problems are your revision list.

</Takeaways>
